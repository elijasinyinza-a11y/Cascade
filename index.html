How it works

From document to executed activity in four steps

Bring what you have. Cascade does the rest.

01

Bring your plan

Upload your workplan, strategic plan, or balanced scorecard — or type a single goal. Cascade reads it and extracts your corporate objectives.

02

Select your goals

Choose which goals to cascade in this session — up to four. Cascade classifies each one by its delivery level before generating anything.

03

Generate the cascade

Cascade builds a six-level chain for each goal — from the boardroom to the daily habits of every individual — with KPIs, owners, and tasks at each level.

04

Push to performance

Export to your performance management system, download as a structured document, or push directly to Karis Performance if your organisation uses it.

What you get

Three views of the same cascade

Every cascade generates three ready-to-use outputs — not just a plan, but an executable system.

Output 01

The cascade chain

A six-level chain for each corporate goal — from the strategic mandate at the top to the daily habits of individuals at the bottom. Every level carries a KPI, an owner, a measurement method, and specific tasks.

Output 02

Department KPIs

Each goal decomposed by department — who owns what, what they measure, what they report. Ready to paste into a performance review template or a team scorecard.

Output 03

Individual contract

A sample individual performance contract for a named role — showing the daily tasks, the frequency, and the line of sight from that person's work all the way back to the corporate goal.

Cascade integrity check — included on every output
Before any output is finalised, Cascade runs a five-point integrity check — verifying that every KPI traces back to the corporate goal, every level has an owner, and every individual task is actionable. Flags are surfaced before you export.
